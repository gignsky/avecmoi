What's changing with UAD 3.6

Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac have replaced the familiar fixed appraisal forms with one dynamic report. A few of the headline changes we'll dig into:

  1. Sep 8, 2025Limited production begins
  2. Jan 26, 2026Broad production — lenders may submit UAD 2.6 or 3.6
  3. Nov 2, 2026UAD 3.6 mandatory for new submissions
  4. May 3, 2027UAD 2.6 fully retired (revisions only until then)

One report, not many forms

The redesigned Uniform Residential Appraisal Report (URAR) replaces the legacy form set and adapts its sections to the property type and assignment.

Far more detail

Room-level information, component-by-component update status, energy-efficiency features, and disaster-mitigation characteristics are now captured as data.

Ratings, split and refined

Quality and condition ratings carry updated definitions and are reported separately for the interior and exterior of the home.

Measured square footage

Expectations for how gross living area is measured and reported are more formal — tax records and builder plans carry even less weight.

New terminology

Familiar labels and definitions have changed. Knowing the new vocabulary makes the new report much easier to read.

What it means for you

Better listing data, complete property details, and an appraiser's package matter more than ever. Bring your questions.
